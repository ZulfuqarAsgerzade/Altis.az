Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, services, and company policies in our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section. Get the information you need quickly and easily.

What materials do you work with?

We work with a wide range of materials including metals, plastics, composites, and more. If you have specific material requirements, please contact us for more information.

Can you handle large-scale production runs?

Yes, we are equipped to manage both small and large-scale production runs. Our facilities are designed to handle high-volume manufacturing efficiently.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a diverse range of industries including automotive, aerospace, construction, medical, and consumer goods.

Is custom fabrication available?

Absolutely, we offer custom fabrication services tailored to your unique specifications. Our team works closely with you to deliver customized solutions.

Do you provide prototyping services?

Yes, we offer prototyping services to help you develop and refine your products before full-scale production. This ensures your designs meet all necessary requirements.

What are your payment terms?

Our payment terms vary depending on the project size and client agreement. Generally, we require a deposit upfront with the balance due upon delivery or completion.
